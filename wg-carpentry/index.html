Services

Most of our work is repairs, trim, and small carpentry jobs around the house. We also mow a handful of lawns for regular customers.

Trim & molding

Baseboard, casing, and crown molding installed or replaced.

Doors & windows

Sticking doors, worn hardware, screens and storm doors repaired or swapped out.

Deck repair

Rotted boards replaced, loose railings reset, decks restained.

Shelving & built-ins

Closet shelving, mudroom benches, simple built-in cabinets.

General repairs

Drywall patches, loose railings, cabinet doors that won't close right — the small stuff that piles up.

Lawn maintenance

Mowing and basic yard upkeep for a handful of regular customers. Ask if we're taking on new lawns.
