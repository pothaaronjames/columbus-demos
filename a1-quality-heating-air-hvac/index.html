What's included in a seasonal tune-up

Book one in spring for your AC and one in fall for your furnace. Takes about twenty minutes.

  • Inspect & clean burner assembly or condenser coil
  • Check refrigerant level and pressure
  • Test thermostat calibration & response
  • Inspect ductwork & connections for leaks
  • Replace or check air filter
  • Verify safety controls & carbon monoxide levels
